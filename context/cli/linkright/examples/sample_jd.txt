Product Manager / Senior Product Manager — Attentive.ai

Location: Noida, India
Type: Full-time

About Attentive.ai:
Attentive.ai is an AI-powered construction technology company that automates takeoff and estimation for construction, landscaping, paving, and field-service companies. Our products, Beam AI and AutoMeasure, use computer vision to extract measurements from blueprints and aerial imagery, validated by a human QA team for 98%+ accuracy.

Role Overview:
We're looking for a Product Manager to own a segment of our product — end to end. You'll drive strategy, execution, and growth for your segment, working closely with engineering, design, data science, and customer success teams.

- Strong analytical skills — SQL, data analysis, A/B testing

- Excellent stakeholder management across engineering, design, and business teams

Should-Have (P1):

Nice-to-Have (P2):

What You'll Do:
