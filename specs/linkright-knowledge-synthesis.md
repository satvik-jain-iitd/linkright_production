## Part 2 — Research Knowledge Base

### 43 Docs — Thematic Map

**Tier 1: Hiring mechanics (10 docs)**
- Resume, LinkedIn, portfolio as perception documents (not records)
- Interview = psychological risk-reduction, not pure rational evaluation
- Behavioral signaling: tone, pacing, composure reduce interviewer uncertainty
- Story construction anchored to: ownership? ambiguity tolerance? judgment? friction risk?

**Tier 2: Career narrative architecture (8 docs)**
- Identity evolution — each seniority level requires different leverage systems
- Archetypal coherence: PM subtypes, founder-mode, CoS, AI-native must be internally consistent
- Consistency chain: resume → LinkedIn → interview → negotiation → reference checks
- Micro-reputation + backchannel dynamics compound silently (Slack, Discord, alumni networks)

**Tier 3: High-leverage operator OS (10 docs)**
- High agency: move independently, solve ambiguously, reduce overhead on others
- Decision-making under uncertainty: probabilistic, progressive, adaptability-preserving
- AI-augmented workflows: AI accelerates cognition, does not replace judgment
- Performance/cognitive optimization: sustainable quality > activity volume

**Tier 4: Ecosystem + relationship navigation (6 docs)**
- Startup/VC ecosystems = compounding trust networks; reputation spreads fast
- Internal advocacy: offers go to candidates someone inside champions strongly
- Referral strategy = trust transfer, not transaction
- Elite networking: quality > volume; become trusted ecosystem node

**Tier 5: Domain-specific leadership tracks (7 docs)**
- PM archetypes: Staff PM (influence without authority), Director (leverage layer), AI Product (trust-aware design), CoS (force multiplier), Founder (speed + ownership + low-ego)
- Negotiation: signal self-awareness + market knowledge, not entitlement
- Case interviews: test reasoning systems, not memorized frameworks
- Async hiring: evaluates clarity, structure, decision-making, remote collaboration fit

**Tier 6: Technical/infrastructure (2 docs)**
- Width optimization: font metrics + pixel budgets > LLM-based bullet optimization
- Application pipeline OS: systematic funnel (positioning → targeting → outreach → prep)

### Gaps in Research Knowledge Base
1. No measurement/feedback loop — "did these frameworks improve outcomes?"
2. No failed-hire RCA methodology — silent rejections have no correction mechanism
3. No salary data by archetype/subtype — negotiation guides exist without market benchmarks
4. No post-offer integration guides — all docs stop at signed offer
5. No crisis recovery playbook — micro-reputation guide exists but no "rebuild after failure"
6. No cross-domain transition guides — IC→PM, PM→founder, PM→executive coach
7. No vertical specialization — healthcare/fintech/AI-infra have different credibility signals
8. No long-tail channel guides — founder cold-outreach, angel→role, advisor→operator transitions
